# Damaged, defective or missing items

Report a problem within 7 days of delivery. Reports after 7 days are still reviewed, but the carrier claim may no longer be possible, so we may offer a goodwill remedy instead of a full claim.

Damaged on arrival: take clear photos of the outer packaging, the damaged item and the shipping label. Keep all packaging until the claim is closed. Send the photos by replying to the ticket or by upload in the portal. For freight, note the damage on the delivery receipt at the time of delivery, because the driver's signature on a clean receipt weakens the claim. We still help if this was not done, but resolution can take longer.

What we offer: for confirmed damage or defects we offer a replacement, a repair where applicable, or a full refund. The customer chooses. Replacements for damaged items ship express whenever stock allows. We open and manage the carrier claim ourselves; the customer never has to deal with the carrier.

Missing items and short deliveries: first check every box and any separate shipment, since orders can be split. If the count is still short, tell us the number of items expected and received and send the delivery receipt or driver slip if there is one. We compare this with the packing record and the proof of delivery. Confirmed shortages are reshipped or refunded within 2 business days of confirmation.

Parcel marked delivered but not received: check with neighbours and the building reception, and look for a notification card. Then contact us; we request the delivery photo and GPS position from the carrier within one business day. If the delivery photo does not show the correct address, we reship or refund without needing further proof.

Defective after first use: electronics and appliances carry a 12-month warranty against manufacturing faults. Send the order number and a short description or video of the fault. Warranty does not cover accidental damage, water damage or wear.

Resolution time: most damage and shortage claims are settled within 3 to 5 business days of receiving all evidence.
