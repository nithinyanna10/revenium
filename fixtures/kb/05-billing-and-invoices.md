# Billing, invoices and charges

Invoices are issued on the first business day of each month for the previous month, and for one-off services on the day they are completed. Each invoice has a unique number, a PDF copy in the portal under Billing, and an emailed copy to the billing contact. A copy of any invoice from the last 24 months can be resent on request; ask with the invoice number or the approximate date and amount.

Payment terms: business invoices are due 30 days after the invoice date. Card payments are charged on the invoice date. Late payments accrue a 1.5 percent monthly fee after a 7-day grace period, which can be waived once per year on request.

Disputing a charge: tell us the invoice number, the line you question and what you expected instead. We review disputes within 3 business days and place a hold on the disputed line so no late fee accrues while it is under review. If the charge was our error, a credit note is issued and applied to the next invoice or refunded to the original method on request.

Duplicate charges: card pre-authorisations can look like a second charge. A pre-authorisation drops off by itself within 3 to 7 business days and is not a real charge. If two settled charges for the same amount appear on the statement, send us the dates and the last four digits of the card; the duplicate is refunded within 5 business days of confirmation.

Taxes: sales tax or VAT is added where required and is shown as its own line. A tax exemption certificate can be uploaded in the portal; it applies to future invoices only and cannot be applied retroactively to invoices already issued.

Subscriptions: a subscription renews automatically on its renewal date. To cancel, give notice at least 3 business days before the renewal date. A cancellation made after renewal ends the subscription at the end of the current period, and the current period is not refunded except where required by law. After cancellation you receive a confirmation email; if you have no such email, the cancellation was not completed.

Promo codes: one code per order, not combinable with other offers, and it must be entered before payment. Codes cannot be added to an order after it is placed.
