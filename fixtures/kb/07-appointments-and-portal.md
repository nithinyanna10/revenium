# Appointments, reminders and the patient portal

Booking and changing appointments: appointments can be booked, moved or cancelled in the patient portal under Appointments, or by phone during opening hours. Changes made at least 24 hours before the start are free. Cancellations inside 24 hours are recorded as late cancellations; the first late cancellation in any 12 months is waived, and later ones may carry a fee set by the clinic.

Reminders: text and email reminders are sent 48 hours and 3 hours before an appointment. They use the time zone saved on the profile, which defaults to the clinic's time zone. If reminders show the wrong time, check the time zone setting under Profile and notification preferences. Changing the time zone affects future reminders only.

Telehealth visits: a video link is emailed and shown in the portal 15 minutes before the visit. Use a current version of Chrome, Safari or Edge, and allow camera and microphone access when the browser asks. If the link shows an error, refresh once, try another browser, and then call the clinic's front desk; staff can move the visit to a phone call. The link works only for the signed-in patient or the approved proxy.

Test results and documents: results appear in the portal once the clinician has released them, which is usually within 2 to 5 business days. Some results are held for clinician review first. A result that has not appeared does not mean it is missing; ask the front desk to confirm its release status. Administrative staff cannot interpret results or give medical advice.

Proxy access: a parent, guardian or caregiver can be given proxy access after the clinic verifies identity and receives the signed authorisation form. Parents of children under 13 can be given access automatically. Proxy access can be reviewed or revoked by the patient at any time.

Records transfer: to send records to another provider, submit a signed release form with the receiving provider's name and fax or secure address. Processing takes up to 10 business days. Copies for the patient's own use are available in the portal or on request.

Referrals: a referral entered by a clinician is visible in the portal under Referrals within one business day. Referral status shows who has to act next: the clinic, the specialist or the insurer.

If a message mentions an emergency or urgent symptoms, tell the patient to call emergency services immediately and do not wait for a reply.
