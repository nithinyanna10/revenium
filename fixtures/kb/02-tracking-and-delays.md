# Tracking a shipment and handling delays

Every shipment has a tracking number, sent by email when it is dispatched. Enter it on the tracking page or in the customer portal to see scan events. Tracking updates arrive each time the parcel is scanned; between hubs there can be a gap of up to 48 hours with no new scan. A parcel that has not been scanned for 48 hours is not necessarily lost.

What the statuses mean: Label Created means the sender has prepared the shipment but the carrier has not collected it yet. In Transit means the parcel is moving between facilities. Out for Delivery means it is on the vehicle and will arrive the same day. Delivery Exception means something prevented delivery, such as an access problem, a wrong address or weather. Held at Depot means the carrier is waiting for further instructions.

Delays: weather, customs inspection and peak-season volume are the most common causes. We do not guarantee delivery dates for standard shipping, but express and booked freight deliveries are covered by our on-time promise. If an express or booked delivery arrives more than one business day late for a reason within our control, the shipping fee is refunded on request.

When to open a trace: if a parcel has had no scan for 5 business days, or is more than 3 business days past its estimated delivery date, we open a carrier trace. A trace takes 3 to 5 business days to resolve. During a trace we do not need the customer to do anything, but we will ask for a confirmed delivery address and a phone number.

Lost shipments: if the carrier cannot locate the parcel after the trace, it is declared lost. We then offer a choice between a replacement shipped at no charge or a full refund including shipping. Replacements are sent express whenever stock allows.

Split orders: an order can leave in several shipments from different warehouses. Each shipment has its own tracking number and its own estimated date. A split shipment does not cost extra, and a missing part of a split order is handled as its own trace without affecting the parts already delivered.

Contact during a delay: reply to the tracking email or write to support with the order or shipment number. Include the tracking number so we do not have to look it up.
