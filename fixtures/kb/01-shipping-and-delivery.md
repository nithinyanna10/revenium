# Shipping and delivery times

Standard shipments are delivered within 3 to 5 business days of the dispatch date. Express shipments arrive in 1 to 2 business days. Freight and pallet shipments follow a booked delivery window, which is confirmed by email on the day of dispatch. Delivery windows are four hours long, and the carrier calls the receiving contact about 30 minutes before arrival.

Business days exclude weekends and public holidays. Orders placed after 2 pm local time are dispatched the next business day. Remote areas, islands and some rural postcodes add 1 to 3 business days; the extra time is shown at checkout.

Changing a delivery address: the address can be changed free of charge until the shipment has been handed to the carrier. After hand-over, a redirect request is possible but not guaranteed and costs a flat redirect fee, which is shown before you confirm. To request a redirect, give us the shipment number and the full new address, including the dock or suite and an on-site contact name.

Rescheduling: a delivery or pickup can be moved once at no charge if the request arrives at least 24 hours before the window opens. Later requests may incur a missed-slot fee charged by the carrier. Same-day changes are handled on a best-effort basis by the dispatch team.

Delivery attempts: the carrier makes up to three attempts. After the third failed attempt the shipment is returned to the depot and held for 7 days. During that time we can arrange one further delivery attempt; after 7 days the parcel is returned to the sender and a refund is processed less the original shipping cost.

Proof of delivery: signature, photo and GPS timestamp are stored for every delivery. We can send a copy of the proof of delivery within one business day of a request. If the proof does not match what you received, tell us and we will open an investigation with the carrier.

Fuel and accessorial charges: fuel surcharges are applied to freight shipments at the percentage published on the rate card at dispatch. Liftgate, residential delivery and appointment delivery are accessorial services, charged only if they were requested or were required at delivery.
