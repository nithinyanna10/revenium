# Returns, exchanges and refunds

Return window: items can be returned within 30 days of delivery if they are unused and in their original packaging. Sale items marked Final Sale, opened hygiene products and personalised items cannot be returned unless they arrive faulty.

How to start a return: ask support for a return label or use the returns page with the order number and the email used at checkout. A prepaid return label is emailed within one business day. If the label has not arrived after one business day, check the spam folder and then ask us to resend it; we can also issue a QR code for drop-off at any partner location.

Refund timing: once the returned parcel is scanned as delivered to our warehouse, inspection takes 2 to 3 business days. The refund is issued to the original payment method straight after inspection. Banks take 5 to 10 business days to show the credit, so the total time from drop-off can reach 15 business days. We email a refund confirmation the moment it is issued; if a customer has no such email, the refund has not been issued yet.

Refund amounts: the item price and the original standard shipping are refunded when the return is due to our error, such as a wrong or faulty item. For change-of-mind returns the item price is refunded and return shipping costs a flat fee deducted from the refund. Express shipping fees are refunded only if the delivery was late.

Exchanges: we ship the replacement as soon as the return is scanned at the carrier, not after inspection, so the customer does not wait for the full cycle. Exchanges for a different price are settled by charging or refunding the difference.

Wrong item sent: report it with a photo of the item and label. We send the correct item express at no charge and a prepaid label for the wrong one. The customer does not need to wait for the wrong item to be returned.

Store credit: a refund can be taken as store credit, which is added instantly and carries a 10 percent bonus. Store credit cannot be converted back into money.

Chargebacks: please contact us before opening a dispute with your bank. We resolve most cases within 3 business days, and a dispute freezes our ability to issue the refund.
