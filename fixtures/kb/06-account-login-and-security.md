# Account access, login and security

Signing in: use the email address on the account and your password. If you have forgotten the password, choose Forgot password on the sign-in page. A reset link is emailed within a few minutes and stays valid for 60 minutes. Only the most recent link works, so if you request several resets, use the newest email and ignore the others.

Reset emails not arriving: check the spam and promotions folders, confirm that the address is the one on the account, and wait 10 minutes before requesting another. Corporate mail filters sometimes block automated mail; ask your IT team to allow the sender domain, or ask us to send the link to a second approved address.

Lock-outs: after 5 failed sign-in attempts the account is locked for 30 minutes. Waiting is the quickest fix, and further attempts during the lock restart the timer. If you cannot wait, support can unlock the account after verifying your identity with two details: the billing contact name and the last order or invoice number.

Two-factor authentication: if you have lost the device that holds your codes, use one of the recovery codes you saved when setting up. Without recovery codes, support removes two-factor authentication after identity verification, which can take up to 2 business days. You are asked to set it up again at the next sign-in.

Changing account details: you can update the name, phone number and delivery addresses in the portal. Changing the account email address requires confirmation links sent to both the old and the new address. If you no longer have access to the old address, support can change it after identity verification.

Adding and removing users: account owners can add users as Viewer, Editor or Admin in the portal under Users, or ask support by giving the person's name, work email and role. Removing a user takes effect immediately. Only the owner can transfer ownership.

Order history: orders placed as a guest are not linked to an account automatically. Support can link them if the order email matches the account email.

Security: we never ask for your password by email or chat. If you suspect someone else used your account, tell us at once and we will lock it and review recent activity.
