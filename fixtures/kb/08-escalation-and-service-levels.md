# Escalation, service levels and gift cards

First response targets: urgent tickets, such as a lost shipment of perishable goods or a locked-out account, receive a first reply within 2 business hours. Standard tickets receive a first reply within 1 business day. A reply is considered complete only when it answers every open question in the ticket.

Resolution targets: standard issues are resolved within 3 business days. Anything that needs a carrier, the warehouse or a clinician is resolved within 5 business days of all evidence being received. We tell the customer the next update date every time we are waiting on a third party.

When to escalate: escalate to a senior agent when a customer has contacted us three or more times about the same issue, when a promised action was missed, when a refund or credit above 250 dollars is needed, or when the customer asks for a manager. The escalation note must include the order or invoice number, a one-line history of what was already done, and what the customer was promised. Escalated tickets get a named owner and a reply within 4 business hours.

Repeat contacts: do not ask the customer to repeat information that is already in the thread. Acknowledge previous contacts, apologise once, and move straight to the action.

Goodwill gestures: agents may offer a credit of up to 25 dollars or free express shipping on the next order to make up for a clear service failure. Larger gestures need senior approval. Never promise a gesture before it has been approved.

Gift cards: a gift card is delivered by email within 15 minutes of purchase, so check the spam folder first. The balance can be checked in the portal by entering the card number and PIN. A card is redeemable at checkout in one or several orders until the balance reaches zero. Cards do not expire, cannot be exchanged for cash, and lost physical cards can be blocked and reissued only with the original order number. If a gift card shows the wrong balance, send the card number, the order it was used on and a screenshot, and we reconcile it within 2 business days.

Communication style: plain language, short paragraphs, the customer's name, no internal jargon, no blame on the customer or on colleagues.
